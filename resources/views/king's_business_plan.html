Executive Summary

KING’S is a digital business ecosystem designed to help businesses connect with customers, discover opportunities, sell products and services, build relationships, and grow.

We believe that running a business should not mean operating alone.

Across Nigeria and Africa, businesses are constantly searching for customers, suppliers, partners, service providers, distributors, ideas and opportunities. Yet much of this activity remains fragmented across social media, messaging apps, physical networks, personal referrals and disconnected platforms.

KING’S brings these opportunities into one ecosystem.

At its foundation, KING’S provides businesses with a digital presence where they can showcase their businesses, products and services — and become discoverable by customers and other businesses.

What the ecosystem will encompass

  • Business discovery
  • Marketplace commerce
  • Business networking
  • Dropshipping
  • Supplier and reseller connections
  • Business services
  • Advertising and promotion
  • Business education
  • Digital tools
  • Payments and transactions
  • Business analytics
  • Community and collaboration

The long-term ambition is simple: to build one of the most useful digital ecosystems for businesses in Africa.

KING’S is not intended to be just another marketplace. It is intended to become infrastructure for business growth.

Vision & Mission

Our Vision

To build a connected business ecosystem where every entrepreneur has access to the people, customers, tools, knowledge and opportunities they need to grow.

We envision a future where a business owner can come to KING’S and, from one place:

  • Find customers
  • Find suppliers
  • Find partners
  • Sell products
  • Offer services
  • Discover opportunities
  • Learn
  • Network
  • Grow

And where customers can discover trusted businesses without having to search endlessly across different platforms.

Our Mission

To empower businesses with the digital infrastructure, connections and opportunities required to build, grow and thrive.

KING’S exists to make business growth more accessible. We want to help small businesses become more visible, help entrepreneurs form meaningful commercial relationships, help suppliers reach new markets, and help customers discover businesses and products more easily.

03

The Problem

Starting a business is difficult. Growing one can be even harder.

A business owner may have a good product but struggle to find customers. Another may have customers but struggle to find reliable suppliers. Another may have inventory but no distribution network.

Another may need a graphic designer, accountant, logistics company, photographer, manufacturer or marketing professional — but have no reliable way to find one. And another may simply need someone who understands the same journey.

Where business activity is scattered today

  • Instagram
  • WhatsApp
  • Facebook
  • TikTok
  • Google
  • Physical markets
  • Personal contacts
  • Business associations
  • Referrals
  • Multiple marketplaces
  • Informal networks

Social media can provide visibility, but it was not built specifically around the complete business journey. Marketplaces can provide commerce, but they generally focus on buying and selling. Professional networks can provide connections, but may not be designed around everyday commerce.

KING’S brings these ideas together.

05

The Solution: Six Layers

KING’S is a business ecosystem built in interconnected layers — each one strengthening the others.

Layer 1

Business Discovery

Businesses create profiles that allow customers and other businesses to discover them.

  • Business name & description
  • Category & subcategory
  • Location
  • Products & services
  • Contact information
  • Social media links
  • Reviews
  • Verification status

This creates a digital identity for businesses.

Layer 2

Marketplace

Businesses showcase and sell products through KING’S. Customers can:

  • Browse products
  • Search by category
  • Discover businesses
  • View product information
  • Contact sellers
  • Place orders
  • Pay directly through KING’S
  • Review businesses

The marketplace turns discovery into commerce.

Layer 3

Business Networking

KING’S allows businesses to discover and connect with other businesses. For example:

  • A fashion brand may need a photographer
  • A restaurant may need a food supplier
  • A retailer may need a distributor
  • A manufacturer may need resellers
  • A startup may need a developer

KING’S creates a digital environment for those relationships.

Layer 4

Dropshipping

KING’S connects suppliers with businesses that want to sell products without maintaining inventory.

  • Suppliers list products available for dropshipping
  • Resellers discover and market those products
  • Resellers earn a margin on each sale

Supplier → Reseller → Customer. KING’S becomes the platform connecting all three.

Layer 5

Business Services

Businesses don’t only need products — they need services. KING’S becomes a place to discover:

  • Accountants & lawyers
  • Marketers & graphic designers
  • Photographers & developers
  • Logistics & fulfilment providers
  • Consultants & recruiters
  • Real estate & event professionals
  • Cleaning & security services
Layer 6

Business Growth

KING’S provides resources that help businesses improve:

  • Business education & courses
  • Guides & webinars
  • Business insights
  • Marketing resources
  • Financial education
  • Entrepreneurship content
  • Business tools

KING’S should not only help businesses sell. It should help them become better businesses.

Business Model

KING’S will develop multiple revenue streams over time.

1. Business Subscriptions

Businesses can subscribe to different membership tiers.

TierDesigned For
BasicBusinesses starting their digital journey.
PremiumBusinesses looking for greater visibility and functionality.
PlatinumBusinesses requiring advanced exposure, tools and opportunities.

Exact pricing and benefits will evolve based on customer behaviour and market validation.

2. Transaction Revenue

As KING’S develops its payment and order infrastructure, the platform can generate revenue through transaction fees. This creates alignment between KING’S and its businesses:

When businesses grow and transact more, KING’S grows with them.

3. Featured Businesses & Products

Businesses can pay for additional visibility, including:

  • Featured businesses
  • Featured products
  • Promotional campaigns
  • Sponsored placements
  • Category promotions

Paid placements will always be transparent and clearly distinguished from organic results.

4. Dropshipping Revenue

  • Supplier subscriptions
  • Dropshipping transaction fees
  • Premium supplier placement
  • Reseller tools
  • Fulfilment services

5. Advertising

As KING’S develops a significant audience, businesses can advertise directly to relevant customers and business audiences.

6. Business Services

Longer term, KING’S can generate revenue through selected business services, partnerships and premium tools.

12

Go-To-Market Strategy

KING’S will initially prioritise business acquisition over mass consumer acquisition.

Why? Because a marketplace is only valuable when there is something worth discovering. The first objective is therefore to build supply.

Stage 1 — Acquire Businesses

Target businesses through:

  • Direct outreach
  • Social media & WhatsApp
  • Business communities
  • Referrals
  • Physical business networks
  • Entrepreneurial events
  • Partnerships
  • Industry associations

Stage 2 — Build Business Density

Instead of spreading randomly across thousands of businesses, KING’S should build strong concentrations within selected categories and locations.

ApproachRelative Value
Lagos → Fashion → 50 strong businessesPotentially more useful initially
Nigeria → 500 scattered businessesLower commercial density

Density creates discoverability and commercial activity.

Stage 3 — Activate Customers

Once enough businesses and products exist:

  • Social media campaigns
  • Search engine optimisation
  • Influencer partnerships
  • Referral campaigns
  • Product discovery content
  • Business stories
  • Promotional campaigns

These drive customers into the ecosystem.

North Star & Final Statement

KING’S should ultimately be measured by one question:

How much more capable is a business because it is part of KING’S?

If the answer is:

  • “I found customers.”
  • “I found a supplier.”
  • “I found a partner.”
  • “I made sales.”
  • “I found someone who could help my business.”
  • “I discovered an opportunity.”
  • “My business became more visible.”

…then KING’S is doing what it was created to do.

KING’S is being built on a simple belief: businesses should not have to grow alone.

The internet has made it easier than ever to reach people. But reaching people is not the same as building relationships. KING’S aims to bridge that gap.

We are building a place where businesses can connect, sell and grow — where entrepreneurs can discover opportunities, customers can discover businesses, suppliers can discover resellers, and relationships can become real commercial value.

Today, KING’S begins with business profiles, products, services and discovery. Tomorrow, it can become a marketplace. Then a business network. Then a dropshipping ecosystem. Then a business services platform.

And ultimately: a connected digital ecosystem for African businesses.

This is KING’S.

Businesses should not have to grow alone.

Connect. Sell. Grow.
